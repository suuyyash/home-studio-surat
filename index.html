Philosophy

The Art of Pure Solitude

"We do not design layouts for cleansing; we craft sanctuaries for reflection. The bathroom is the single space in the modern home where noise ceases, where water becomes therapy, and where craftsmanship speaks in quiet tones."

At Home Studio, Surat, our curation values the physical tactile response of brushed metals, the silent engineering of smart toilets, the scent of Canadian hemlock wood sauna cabins, and the visual weight of book-matched marble textures. We believe your bath space is a physical reflection of your life's appreciation for fine details.

Since 2001

From Studio Ceramic to Home Studio

Founded as Studio Ceramic in 2001 within an intimate 800 sqft boutique space, our business was founded on a commitment to uncompromising material quality and elite aesthetics.

Driven by client demand for global trends, we expanded our footprint in 2011 to a sprawling showroom, re-branding as Home Studio. Today, with a luxury carpet area of 19,500 sqft, we supply materials to developers, interior designers, and architects all over Gujarat and India.

Services

Bespoke Architectural Consultations

Luxury requires precision planning. Home Studio maintains a dedicated in-house technical team working alongside developers, custom home builders, and interior designers to manage layouts smoothly.

We assist with water pressure calculations for multi-head rain showers, coordinate concealed valve spacing, design custom steam and sauna wood layouts, and compile detailed materials specifications sheets, reducing errors before construction.
