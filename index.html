Philosophy

The Art of Pure Solitude

"We don't design bathrooms; we craft sanctuaries for reflection. From refined tiles and elegant windows to smart sanitary ware and precision fittings, every detail is designed to turn everyday spaces into moments of calm, comfort, and quiet luxury."

At Home Studio, Surat, we curate tiles, architectural windows, sanitary ware, and bathroom fittings that celebrate exceptional design. From tactile finishes and smart technology to natural textures and refined details, every element is thoughtfully selected to create spaces that reflect your appreciation for quality and craftsmanship.

Since 2001

From Studio Ceramic to Home Studio

Home Studio is a retail organization that was earlier known as "Studio Ceramic", established in the year 2001 with an area of around 800 sqft. In 2011 the area was extended to 17,000 sqft and was re-named as "Home Studio" with a current carpet area of 19,500 sqft.

Since its establishment, Home Studio prides itself in superior material quality, supplying goods to retail customers, interior designers, builders, and architects all over Gujarat and India. Our eye for only the most supreme quality has earned us the ability to supply material to some of the most prestigious locations and projects. Home Studio has evolved into a premium lifestyle destination synonymous with innovation and exclusivity.

We also concentrate on business from leading Developers, Designers & Architects. We have a specialized team to deal with all small and big projects.
