Our History

The Growth of Home Studio

2001

Studio Ceramic Foundations

Established as "STUDIO CERAMIC" in a modest 800 sqft boutique. Our primary goal was to bring premium imported tiles and select sanitaryware to Surat, focusing strictly on material durability.

2011

The Showroom & Re-Branding

To display full wet-demonstration models, we relocated to Udhana-Magdalla Road, expanding to a 17,000 sqft facility and re-naming ourselves as "HOME STUDIO". We added premium CP fittings, faucets, and wellness solutions to our curation.

Present

Surat's Premier 19,500 Sqft Studio

Now standing with a carpet area of 19,500 sqft, Home Studio serves as a landmark lifestyle destination, supplying materials to builders, interior architects, and custom projects all over India.

Leadership

Our Founders

The visionaries behind Home Studio's transition into India's leading luxury sanitation curator.

Founder & Managing Director

Rajesh Patel

Rajesh founded Studio Ceramic in 2001, setting the standard for material logistics and sourcing in Gujarat. His focus on building authentic manufacturer relationships directly translates to Home Studio's ability to supply major real estate developers with high volumes.

Founder & Design Director

Sanjay Patel

Sanjay joined during the 2011 transition, driving the design curation, imports library, and the installation of Surat's pressurized demonstration center. He works directly with interior architects, selecting luxury layouts that match custom client needs.

Vision & Purpose

Vision & Mission

Our vision is to serve as the ultimate destination for luxury bath and home interiors in India, recognized for absolute reliability, curating global design trends, and building lasting relationships.

Our mission is to empower architects, designers, and homeowners with premium materials, flawless aesthetics, and interactive live demonstration systems that remove guessing and elevate living spaces.

Curation

Supreme Material Curation

Over the last two decades, our eye for supreme quality has earned us the ability to supply materials to some of the most prestigious locations and high-value developer projects in India.

We work closely with retail homeowners, custom builders, renowned interior architects, and prominent real estate developers.

Values

Supreme Quality
